## Problem

The Bucket Meter, a waterproof IoT weighing device, read 300–400 g off. The cause was not obvious, and the device had to keep its IP68 water and dust protection.

## Approach

- As interim lead mechanical engineer, traced the error to the oil seal: it pressed against the load path and absorbed part of the force before it reached the load cell.
- Designed and cast an in-house silicone seal that keeps water out without loading the measurement path.

## Result

The error dropped from **300–400 g to 50–60 g**, and the device kept its **IP68** rating.
