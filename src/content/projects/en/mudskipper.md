## Problem

Surveillance in shallow, muddy coastal areas needs a robot that can move both on land and in water. Wheeled robots get stuck, and boats cannot cross land.

## Approach

- Took inspiration from the mudskipper, a fish that walks on its fins, to design a robot that both crawls and swims.
- Fitted an ESP32-CAM for live video and added autonomous obstacle avoidance.

## Result

A working amphibious prototype that moves on land and in water and streams live video. This was one of the first robots I built.
