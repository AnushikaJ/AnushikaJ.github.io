## Problem

The Stall Monitor, a camera device used in horse stables, ran hot, which put reliability and component life at risk.

## Approach

- Designed a new heat sink and checked it with thermal simulation.
- Confirmed the simulated result with physical tests on real hardware.

## Result

Heat dropped by **about 50%**. The work is now continuing towards a passive cooling solution.
