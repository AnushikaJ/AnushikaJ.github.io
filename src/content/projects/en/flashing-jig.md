## Problem

The existing flashing jig worked, but it was expensive to build, which made extra jigs for scaling production costly.

## Approach

- Redesigned the jig around a pogo-pin bed and a single toggle-clamp lever, so one motion seats the board and makes contact.
- Kept a status display on the base and routed the wiring cleanly to the programming electronics.
- Replaced costly parts with simpler machined aluminium and acrylic plates without losing alignment accuracy.

## Result

Production cost of the jig dropped by **more than 75%**, so extra stations for the line are now cheap to build.
