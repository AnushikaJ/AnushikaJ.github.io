## Problem

Conventional gem faceting machines offer only about 96 index positions. That limits which cut geometries a craftsman can produce, and how precisely.

## Approach

- Led a nine-person team through the design and build of a new faceting and polishing machine.
- Designed an indexing mechanism with control over every position from 1 to 360.

## Result

- Facet indexing across **1–360 positions**, about **3.75×** the resolution of conventional machines.
- Shown at **FACETS Sri Lanka 2025** and recognised by the **President of Sri Lanka**.
- **Patent pending**, filed by the university.
