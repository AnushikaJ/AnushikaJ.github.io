## Problem

Every Bucket Meter (an IP68 IoT weighing device) had to be calibrated, registered, flashed with firmware and NFC-tagged before shipping. Each step was done separately by hand, which was slow and easy to get wrong.

## Approach

- Designed a single station that does all four steps in one automated sequence, with a touchscreen interface and an emergency stop.
- Built the frame from machined aluminium, with a reference-weight stack and a guided loading fixture so every device sits in the same position.
- Took the jig from concept to a working prototype in six weeks as an intern, then commissioned it on the production line.

## Result

Each device now runs calibration, registration, flashing and NFC writing in **under 12 minutes**. The jig is the standard process on the line.
